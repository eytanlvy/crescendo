# 1) TERMS — one per line. They are sent to Whisper as its initial prompt (in this order, up to ~224 tokens:

#    Add your project names, people, tools, libraries, acronyms: anything Whisper keeps getting wrong.
# 2) REPLACEMENTS — "heard => written", applied last, case-insensitive, whole words only.
#    To spot recurring mistakes: keep_history = true in config.toml, then `bin/dictate history -v`.
